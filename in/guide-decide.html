1. Decide Dropper kya hai

Ek web tool jo chat screenshots, handwritten notes, whiteboard ki photo, aur minutes ya committee report jaise documents se text extract karta hai, phir use “Jo tay hua”, “Jo abhi tay nahi hua”, “Karne wale kaam” aur “Record” mein baant deta hai. “Aakhir tay kya hua?” dhoondhne ke liye peeche scroll karne ka time bachta hai.

Kuch install nahi karna — browser mein hi chalta hai. Dropper ka koi server nahi hai aur aapka data kabhi nahi rakhta. Sirf padhna aur copy karna ho to Google sign-in bhi nahi chahiye.

2. Shuru karne se pehle

Ye cheezein chahiye. Event Dropper aur Schedule Dropper se alag, ye tool sirf AI mode mein chalta hai. Kuch bhi padhne ke liye Gemini API key zaroori hai.

3. Basic flow

1Record daalein

Chat screenshot ya meeting notes ko screen par bane area mein drag karein. “Files chunein” dabakar bhi chun sakte hain.

Ek saath kai files daal sakte hain (max 10). Order mein rakhein. Overlap ho to bhi theek hai.

2Tareekh daalein, phir “Padhein” dabayein

“Is record ki tareekh” check karke “Padhein” dabayein. Jis din screenshot liya tha, ya jis din notes likhe the, wo din daalein.

Yahi tareekh “Next Tuesday” jaisi baaton ko asli date banati hai. Ye galat hui to nikalne wali dates bhi galat aayengi.

3Chaar groups check karein

Jo padha gaya wo “Jo tay hua”, “Jo abhi tay nahi hua”, “Karne wale kaam” aur “Record” mein baant kar dikhta hai. Yahan zaroor check karein. Har tareekh ke saath original shabd bhi dikhte hain, to dono ko wahin mila sakte hain.

4Summary copy karein ya image save karein

“Summary copy karein” se sorted result seedha chat ya email mein paste karne layak mil jaata hai. “Image save karein” use ek picture bana deta hai. Dono ke liye sign-in nahi chahiye.

5Deadline wale items calendar mein daalein

“Calendar mein daalein” deadline wale items ko Google Calendar mein daal deta hai. Sign-in sirf yahin maanga jaata hai.

Sirf deadline wale items jaate hain. “Record (ho chuka)” calendar mein kabhi nahi jaata.

“Record (ho chuka)” ek hi group hai jo kabhi calendar tak nahi pahunchta. Wo aage aane wali baat nahi hai, isliye sirf screen par aur copy ki gayi summary mein rehta hai.

5. Result kaise padhein

Reading perfect nahi hai — dates aur handwriting mein hi zyada galti hoti hai. Copy karne ya calendar mein daalne se pehle result zaroor check karein.

Har tareekh ko original shabdon se milayein

Har tareekh ke saath original shabd aate hain, jaise “Next Tuesday”. Isse pata chalta hai ki AI ne sahi asli date banayi ya nahi. “Koi tareekh nahi di gayi” ka matlab hai ki record mein tareekh thi hi nahi.

Handwriting galat padhi ja sakti hai

Handwritten notes aur whiteboard ki photo chalti hai, lekin ghisi-pisi likhaayi galat padhi ja sakti hai. Mushkil ho to acchi roshni mein dobara photo lein, ya text type karke uska screen daalein.

Kai files ho to order maayne rakhta hai

Ek saath kai files daalein to unhe usi order mein rakhein jis order mein record hui thin. Tool baatcheet ke silsile ko dekhkar baantta hai, isliye order badal jaye to “tay hua” aur “abhi tay nahi hua” ka faisla bigad sakta hai. Thoda overlap hona koi problem nahi.

6. Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Kya sign-in karna zaroori hai?
Padhne, copy karne ya image save karne ke liye nahi. Google sign-in sirf tab maanga jaata hai jab aap “Calendar mein daalein” dabate hain.
Jo baatcheet aur notes main daalta hoon, wo kahin save hote hain?
Nahi. Dropper ka koi server nahi hai. Na images aur na hi unse padha gaya kuch hum tak pahunchta hai. Wo sirf aapki apni key par chalne wale Google ke Gemini API tak jaata hai. Detail ke liye privacy policy dekhein.
Dusron ki baatcheet daalna theek hai?
Free tier par, jo aap bhejte hain wo Google ki services improve karne mein use ho sakta hai (paid tier par nahi). Naam wale notes ya minutes daalne se pehle ye dekh lein.
Reading galat aayi
Har tareekh ke saath original shabd dikhte hain, to dono milayein. Handwriting galat padhi ja sakti hai. Dobara photo lena, ya files ko chote batch mein baantna, aksar kaam karta hai.
Kitni files daal sakte hain?
Ek baar mein max 10. Total size bahut bada ho to ruk jaata hai, tab files kam kar dein.
Fax aur PDF chalte hain?
Haan. PDF ke alawa chat screenshots, handwritten notes, whiteboard ki photo, aur minutes ya committee report jaise documents bhi chalte hain.

7. Kuch galat ho to

Price kam rakhne ke liye hum one-on-one support nahi dete. Kuch kaam na kare to is page ke steps ek baar phir dekh lein. Record na padhe to files ko chote batch mein baantna, ya acchi roshni mein dobara photo lena, aksar kaam karta hai.
